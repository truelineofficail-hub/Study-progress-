# 📚 Mission October 2026 — Study Progress Tracker

A minimal, distraction-free **study companion web app** built to help you plan, track, and stay consistent with your daily study mission. Track subjects, chapters, study time, streaks, mock tests, and more — all in one clean dashboard.

🔗 **Live App:** [truelineofficail-hub.github.io/Study-progress-](https://truelineofficail-hub.github.io/Study-progress-/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **🔥 Daily Mission Tracker** — Set a daily study goal (e.g., 2 hours) and track progress in real time.
- **📊 Subject-wise Progress** — Track Computer Science, Political Science, Business Studies, Data Entry, and English separately, with chapter-level tracking.
- **📅 45-Day Mission Plan** — Day-by-day roadmap toward your exam goal.
- **⏱️ Study Timer** — Focus sessions with start/pause/reset, logged automatically per subject.
- **🧠 PYQ Center & Mistake Bank** — Practice previous year questions and revisit your mistakes before the exam.
- **🎯 Mock Tests** — Log scores and track your mock test history over time.
- **🏆 Achievements & Streaks** — Visual motivation for showing up every day.
- **📔 Study Journal** — Daily reflection on what you learned, what was hard, and what to revise.
- **🎯 Target Marks** — Set a target score per subject and track your average.
- **📅 Exam Countdown** — Days remaining for each paper, with subject exam codes.
- **🗒️ Notes** — A personal revision notebook.
- **⚙️ Settings** — Export/import your progress data, reset data, and customize your daily goal.

## 📱 Install on Your Phone (Works Offline Like an App!)

This app is a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** — meaning you can install it directly to your phone's home screen and use it like a native app, without needing the Play Store or App Store.

### 📲 Android (Chrome)
1. Open the [live app link](https://truelineofficail-hub.github.io/Study-progress-/) in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **three-dot menu (⋮)** in the top right corner.
3. Tap **"Add to Home screen"** (or **"Install app"**).
4. Confirm by tapping **"Add"** / **"Install"**.
5. The app icon will appear on your home screen — tap it to open like any other app.

### 🍏 iPhone / iPad (Safari)
1. Open the [live app link](https://truelineofficail-hub.github.io/Study-progress-/) in **Safari** (must be Safari, not Chrome).
2. Tap the **Share icon** (square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right corner.
5. The app icon will now appear on your home screen.

Once installed, it opens full-screen without browser bars, and your progress data stays saved on your device.

## 💾 Data & Privacy

- All progress is stored **locally on your device**.
- Use the **Export My Progress** option in Settings to back up your data.
- Use **Import Progress** to restore your data on another device.
- **Reset Everything** in Settings will permanently clear all saved progress — use with caution.
